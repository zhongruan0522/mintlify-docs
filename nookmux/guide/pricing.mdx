---
title: Pricing
---

The public pricing page shows the current instance's public model catalog and price information. It is available from the top navigation **Pricing** or the `/pricing` route.

When model performance metrics are enabled, model cards show performance badges (latency, throughput, success rate) and the model detail dialog offers a **Performance** tab with window KPIs, a per-group performance table and first-token latency and success rate trend charts. Models with context-segment prices expand a per-group tiered price table in their details.

Pricing data comes from model metadata, model prices, model ratios, and group ratios. Administrators can manage it in:

- **System Settings → Billing & Payments → Pricing Settings**: the **Model prices** tab manages model prices, ratios, and context segment prices; the **Tool prices** tab manages tool call prices; the **Group prices** tab manages group ratios, the auto group, and user-selectable groups.
- **Model Management**: model metadata, vendor info, visibility, and deployment settings.

If the instance enforces restrictions such as "login required to view models/pricing", follow the current access policy.
