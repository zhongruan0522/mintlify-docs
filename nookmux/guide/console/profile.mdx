---
title: Profile
---

The `/profile` page is used to view account information and configure notification settings. Sign-in methods, Passkeys, two-factor authentication, access tokens, and other security features are on the Security & Access page (`/profile/security`). Both pages are reachable from the **Personal** section of the sidebar and from the avatar menu in the top-right corner. The interface language is switched with the language switcher in the header and only applies to the current browser.

Main areas of the profile page:

- **Account Overview**: view your avatar, display name, role, username, email, and group, along with three metrics: current balance, total usage, and API requests.
- **Notification Settings**: choose one notification method (`email`, `webhook`, `bark`, or `gotify`), fill in the fields required by that method, and set the quota warning threshold. Click **Save Changes** in the page header to apply.
- **Daily Check-in**: if the administrator enables check-in, a check-in panel appears at the bottom of the page for checking in and viewing the monthly calendar, total check-ins, and rewards.

Main areas of the Security & Access page:

- **Sign-in & Authentication**: change your password; bind the email, GitHub, or LinuxDO accounts enabled on the site. Each method is a panel of equal width, three per row on wide screens, wrapping onto new rows as methods are added.
- **Passkey Login**: register, rename, and remove Passkeys.
- **Two-Factor Authentication**: enable or disable 2FA and regenerate backup codes.
- **Access Tokens**: generate and manage your API access token.
- **Delete Account**: at the bottom of the page, with a high-risk warning above it.

Sensitive operations such as deleting your account, generating access tokens, binding an email, and enabling 2FA first prompt a step-up verification dialog (`POST /api/verify`), requiring security verification via 2FA, Passkey, or password.

After you change your password, disable 2FA, or regenerate backup codes, the server invalidates every session of the account, including those in other browsers and devices: the page explains why and returns you to the sign-in page, and you land back on the page you were on after signing in again. When regenerating backup codes, the new codes are shown first and the sign-in page comes only after you close the dialog.

Available bindings and security features depend on what administrators enable in **System Settings → Authentication**.
